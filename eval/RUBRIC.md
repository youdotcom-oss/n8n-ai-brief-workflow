# Labeling Rubric

Written before labeling. Each article is labeled **for the topic it was found under**, from the article's title, description, URL, and date only. Jev's scores were hidden while labeling.

**Audience:** a team of developers building AI agents and AI-powered products, reading a morning brief.

## Label 1: Include in the Brief

All three must hold:

1. **One specific development.** The page reports a single event. It isn't a roundup, tracker, digest, topic page, changelog, or listing of many items.
2. **On topic.** The event fits the topic the article was found under.
   - *AI model releases*: a new AI model is released or made available.
   - *AI agent tooling*: a framework, SDK, platform, or developer tool for building agents is launched or significantly updated.
   - *AI regulation*: a law is passed, signed, or takes effect, a regulator issues final rules, guidance, or enforcement, a court rules, or a head of government signs a binding or formal policy action.
3. **Material and recent.** It matters to the audience and happened in the search window. Old news re-surfaced as new doesn't count.

## Label 0: Leave Out

- Roundups, trackers, "AI updates today", newsletters, topic, tag, and section pages, changelogs, company news indexes
- Opinion, commentary, explainers, compliance guides, predictions, listicles, trend and data stories
- Proposals: bills introduced, plans announced, consultations, hearings
- Safety incidents and company research posts that aren't a release (under any topic)
- End-user products that happen to use agents (for example, a trading app's agents) under *AI agent tooling*
- Real news filed under the wrong topic (for example, an agent product under *AI model releases*)
- Spam, unrelated content, paywall stubs with no indication of the event

## Known Judgment Calls

- **A single story covering two launches** (for example, "Anthropic and OpenAI announce new models") counts as one development: 1.
- **Law-firm "key takeaways" on an announcement** follow the event. If the event is only a plan, the label is 0.
- **Per-topic labeling** means the same real story can be 1 under one topic and 0 under another. That matches how the template groups briefs.
